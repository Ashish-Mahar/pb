About Piyush

Piyush Bhatt is a young singer from Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand, known for blending traditional Garhwali and Kumaoni folk with a fresh, modern touch.

Since his early performances, he has been sharing the music, stories, and soul of the Himalayas with a growing audience — both online and on stage.

His voice carries the warmth of the hills, and his songs celebrate the culture of Uttarakhand.
